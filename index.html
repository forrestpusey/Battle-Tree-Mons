Badge Guide & Rules

B Bronze: Max streak of 30-39 wins.
S Silver: Max streak of 40-49 wins.
G Gold: Max streak of 50-99 wins.
D Diamond: The ultimate tier. Max streak of 100+.
★ Veteran: Has participated in 6 or more recorded runs.
A Ace: Exceptional consistency. Requires a 45+ Rating and at least 3 runs.
⬥ Specialist: Versatility master. Has been paired with at least 9 unique teammates.
L Legacy: A true legend. Has accumulated over 500 total wins across all history.
